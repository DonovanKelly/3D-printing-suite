Optional top loops and supported rear keyholes share the same geometry pipeline in the browser and CLI; preview and exports use the finished extents for build-plate checks.

The built-in Alpine sample now has an enclosing curved crest, so selecting Image contour works immediately, including with no bezel and zero padding. Disconnected uploads still produce an actionable error, and Use sample artwork restores a valid downloadable project. Structural contour repair preserves detailed SVG negative space, and the 3D viewer redraws when the scene or camera changes.
